How to turn a find into an order

  1. Save the seller listing. Open a CAFANS product and follow its original listing link when available. Confirm that your chosen agent accepts the seller URL.
  2. Compare measurements. For tees, record chest width and body length from a garment you own. For shoes, check whether the seller’s chart gives foot length or insole length.
  3. Review the exact variant. Check color and size availability. If your agent offers warehouse QC photos, compare the label, visible condition and requested measurements with the order.
  4. Get a shipping quote. Confirm the destination, parcel dimensions, billable weight and any separately charged fees before paying for international delivery.

PikoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the product links go?

They open CAFANS product pages. Check the original seller link on the product page and confirm compatibility with PikoBuy before ordering. This is an independent shopping guide.

Does the listed USD price include shipping?

The catalog shows the estimated item price. Enter local delivery, international shipping, applicable taxes and agent fees separately in the budget worksheet.

Where can I check QC photos?

Ask your shopping agent which warehouse photo options it offers. Request a clear photo of any detail that affects your decision, such as a size label or a garment measurement.
